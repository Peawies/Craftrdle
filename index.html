Craftrdle

CraftersMC item guessing game

How to Play

Guess the secret CraftersMC item by entering item names and comparing the item features. You have 10 guesses to find the correct item.

Item Type Tip - Items related to minions or pets will say CLOSE for the item type for each other. For example, if you have to guess Small Minion Backpack, and you guess Foul Flesh, it'll say CLOSE since they are both minion items.

Obtaining Tip - Similar to the previous tip, items whose recipes are from collections will say CLOSE for the obtaining hint for each other. For example, if you have to guess Revenant Falchion which is a Slayer collection item, and you guess Farmer Orb which is a Farming collection item, it'll say CLOSE since they are both connected to a collection.
